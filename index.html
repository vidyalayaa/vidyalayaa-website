Features

Everything your school needs,
in one platform

From NFC attendance to parent notifications, academic management to fee tracking — Vidyalayaa brings it all together.

NFC Smart Attendance

Students tap their NFC cards at school gates. Arrival and departure are recorded instantly, with parents notified in real-time. No manual roll calls needed.

Real-Time Parent Alerts

Push notifications, SMS, and email alert parents the moment their child arrives or departs. Full attendance history available on mobile.

Academic Tracking

Manage classes, exams, marks, homework, and timetables. Teachers enter results that parents can view instantly by subject and exam.

Role-Based Dashboards

Tailored views for Admins, Principals, Teachers, Parents, and Students — each seeing exactly what they need, nothing more.

Fee Management

Track fee records, generate receipts, and give parents clear visibility into pending and paid fees — all from a single dashboard.

Reports & Analytics

School-wide analytics on attendance patterns, academic performance, and operational metrics. Export reports in CSV or PDF with one click.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

NFC (Near Field Communication) attendance uses small, durable NFC cards issued to each student. When a student taps their card on our device at the school gate, their arrival time is instantly recorded and parents are notified in real-time. The device works offline too — it stores taps locally and syncs when internet is available.

Absolutely. Vidyalayaa uses enterprise-grade security: all data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest, multi-tenant isolation ensures one school can never access another's data, and role-based access control limits who can see what. We also support MFA for admin accounts and maintain comprehensive audit logs.

Our NFC devices have a durable local queue. If internet goes down, the device continues recording taps locally with timestamps. Once connectivity is restored, all queued events are automatically uploaded in idempotent batches — no tap is ever lost.

Yes! Parents can have children across multiple schools on Vidyalayaa. Each child-school combination creates a separate membership, and parents can switch between them seamlessly in the portal. Notifications come for all linked children.

Most schools are up and running within a week. School registration and configuration takes about a day, NFC device installation takes 1-2 days, and student card issuance runs in parallel. Our team provides hands-on onboarding support throughout.

Vidyalayaa supports five primary roles: School Admin (full school management), Principal (school-wide view), Teacher (scoped to assigned classes/subjects), Parent (view linked children), and Student (view own records). Each role has granular permission controls enforced both in the UI and on the server.
